AI product intern, Vitalis

Alex and Call Assist

Vitalis sells payment systems to restaurants. I was hired to build a website. From my desk I kept hearing the sales reps say "hold on, let me find that" while they flipped through a printed packet.

So I built Call Assist. It listens to a live sales call and puts the right answer on the rep's screen the second a customer pushes back. The owner had tried to fix this before and it had not worked. He told me to give it a shot.

Then I built Alex, an AI sales rep that makes the first call itself. It can switch languages in the middle of a conversation, book an appointment, and hand the customer to a live rep when they are ready.

Intern, IMpowered Fitness

IMpowered Fitness

I spent this past summer interning at a gym. I called more than 200 leads, worked on where the gym shows up on Google, and stickered 500 water bottles for a World Cup watch party in Tustin.

The part I am proudest of started as a question. How do you get members to come in more often? I pitched the owner a screen on the gym floor that shows every member's check ins and streaks. Once people could see a streak, skipping a day felt like losing something.

He put it on more TVs. Now there is a strength board, a workout of the day board, and one at the front desk. After my last day he texted me a job offer.

CEO, Modjewel

Virtual Enterprise

Virtual Enterprise is a class where students run a company for the year. Ours is Modjewel. We sell modular jewelry, which means pieces you can swap in and out.

I am the CEO. A lot of people expect the CEO to decide everything. I run it the other way. I ask for opinions, we vote, and I call on the quiet people on purpose.

When a meeting goes silent, I stop and ask each person what they think. I learned that coaching six year olds. They show you they are bored by lying down in the grass. Teenagers just go quiet.
